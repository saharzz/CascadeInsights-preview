A Seattle-based statistical strategy partner

We work with lean, ambitious teams, from start-ups and academic spin-offs to mission-driven research organizations and nonprofits, who often navigate uncharted territory, with higher risks than large corporations. As researchers, we embrace it, because it signals questions of substance worth exploring. We work with you to make thoughtful and rigorous decisions with the best information available and refine them as evidence accumulates. We are an agile partner who upholds your vision and mission, not a transactional vendor, whether as an independent strategic advisor, a fractional statistical leader, or an expert resource for specific statistical tasks. See how we work and what we offer.

Our mission is to help ambitious teams and communities advance science and improve lives through rigorous and relevant statistics, and the capacity to carry it forward.

Our principals

Sahar Zangeneh, PhD (she/her)

Founder and principal consultant

Sahar designs and analyzes trials, surveys, and observational studies, and develops methods for missing data and selection bias. She teaches at the University of Washington and is a statistical editor for Nature Medicine.

Sahar Zangeneh (she/her) is a (bio)statistician specializing in the design and analysis of experimental and observational studies, including complex sample surveys, individual- and community-randomized trials, and observational cohort studies. Her methodological work focuses on developing statistical methods for addressing missingness and non-random selection, particularly when missingness or selection depends on unobserved factors (missing not at random). Her collaborative research spans across translational, clinical and population-level studies with a focus on health equity. She is a Clinical Associate Professor at the University of Washington, where she teaches graduate courses and mentors students. She is a statistical editor for Nature Medicine and elected chair of the American Statistical Association's Committee on Applied Statisticians.

Ali Shojaie, PhD (he/him)

Co-founder and principal consultant

Ali works at the intersection of statistical machine learning and network analysis, with applications in biology and the social sciences. He is an endowed professor at the University of Washington and a statistical editor for NEJM AI.

Ali Shojaie (he/him) is the Norm Breslow Endowed Professor of Biostatistics and Statistics at the University of Washington, where he serves as Interim Chair of the Department of Biostatistics. His research lies at the intersection of statistical machine learning, statistical network analysis, and applications in biology and social sciences. He is the founding director of the Summer Institute for Statistics in Big Data (SISBID) at the University of Washington and Lead of the Data Management and Statistics (DMS) Core of the UW Alzheimer's Disease Research Center (ADRC). He is the editor of the New England Journal of Statistics in Data Science and a statistical editor for NEJM AI. He has chaired over 20 doctoral dissertations and advised numerous postdocs and junior researchers.

How we work together

We are University of Washington faculty and educators at heart, and together we bring half a century of classroom experience and over three decades of collaborative research. This experience lets us offer pragmatic solutions that meet your needs without sacrificing rigor. We explain our reasoning, not just our results. We are naturally curious and enjoy learning about your science, so we learn and grow together.

Experience

Our name

Our name pays homage to the Cascades and reflects three principles at the core of our practice: decisions cascade downstream; like the peaks of the range, each study is distinct and calls for its own approach; and impact comes from collaboration, just as the range shapes the climate together.
